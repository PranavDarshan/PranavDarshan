## 🚀 About Me

I'm a Computer Science Engineering graduate from **RV College of Engineering**, currently working as a **Cloud Developer I at Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE Aruba)**. My interests lie at the intersection of AI reliability, mechanistic interpretability, and cloud systems.

I enjoy building end-to-end systems, investigating how machine learning models behave, and exploring ways to make AI more reliable and trustworthy.

- 🔬 **Research:** Working on hallucination detection, diffusion language models, RAG reliability, and AI interpretability.
- 📄 **Publications:** Research published in IEEE Xplore, with papers accepted at the NeurIPS Global South AI Workshop and UncertaiNLP at EMNLP 2026.
- 💼 **Experience:** Cloud Developer I at HPE Aruba, with previous experience in cloud development, enterprise systems, and LiDAR perception research.
- 🏆 **Achievements:** Award of Merit at The Open Group INITIATE Enterprise Architecture Competition 2025; 1st Place at the IEEE Hackathon on AI in Education.
- 🎓 **Academics:** Graduated from RV College of Engineering with a CGPA of 9.66/10.

## 📝 Research & Publications

**The Detectability Gap: Hidden Heterogeneity in Hallucination Detection Across Language Models**  
*Accepted at NeurIPS 2026 Global South AI Workshop*

- Investigates why sampling consistency can reveal some hallucinations while concealing others.
- Characterizes different hallucination detectability regimes, including consistently incorrect and inconsistent model responses.

**The Temporal Tug-of-War: Visualizing and Detecting RAG Conflicts in Diffusion Models via Trajectory Variance**  -- [Link](https://github.com/PranavDarshan/temporal-tug-of-war_)

*Accepted at UncertaiNLP Workshop, EMNLP 2026*

- Investigates conflicts between parametric knowledge and retrieved evidence in diffusion language models.
- Introduces Trajectory Variance Score (TVS) to study semantic divergence across independent denoising trajectories.

**Leveraging LLM and RAG for Automated Answer Script Evaluation**  -- [Link](https://github.com/PranavDarshan/AutoGrader)

*IEEE CSITSS 2024 | IEEE Xplore*

- Explores the application of large language models and retrieval-augmented generation for automated educational assessment.
